Place ? Event markers (Terrain > Events). When an ally next to one clicks Interact, a big throw button pops up over the table (only you see it and the die); the event card with the rules and helper rolls then shows in the same spot.

Random floor

Builds rooms joined by corridors on the picked board, with stairs in the room furthest from the team. Players only see the room they start in: corridors light up as allies walk, and a room appears when someone reaches its doorway. A player standing on the Stairs gets a "Go to the next floor" button: the whole team goes down to a fresh floor. Pokémon names that match the site get their stats.

Terrain

While painting, click or drag on the board to place tiles. Minis can't be moved until you turn painting off.

Saved boards

Keeps the picked board's size, picture, terrain and GM minis. Players never see this list.

Layers

Map out several floors at once: only you see layers other than the one the team is on. Stairs on the team's layer take everyone to the next layer.
