Settings

Point PixelDex at your Minecraft world's save folder to track what you've caught automatically.

Pick the folder named after your world (it has a "playerdata" folder inside it), e.g. …\Instances\<modpack>\saves\<world name>. PixelDex only reads it — nothing is changed.
