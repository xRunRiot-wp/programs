Schedule Helper

Get your HotSchedules schedule into Kronos without typing it all by hand.

Short on time?

The one-page QUICKSTART (in the same folder) has just the weekly steps. This guide has the details and pictures.

Before you start

  • It's an add-on for Google Chrome on a computer. It also works in Microsoft Edge. It doesn't work on a phone.
  • It never sees or saves your password, and it never signs in for you. You sign in to Kronos yourself, as usual.
  • The schedule stays on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Nothing is saved in Kronos until you say so.
Please check with your manager first that it's OK to use a helper like this on Kronos and on your work computer. Your company's computer rules still apply. If they'd rather you didn't use auto-fill, Copy mode works on its own: it's just copy and paste, and you do every click.

A. Set it up (one time only)

  1. Download schedule-helper.zip from the link Joseph sent you.
  2. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Put the folder somewhere it can stay, for example Documents\ScheduleHelper. Chrome uses this folder every time, so don't delete it.
  3. In Chrome, click the address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Enter. (In Edge, type edge://extensions.)
  4. Turn on Developer mode using the switch in the top right corner. In Edge the switch is on the left.
  5. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder inside the folder you extracted.
  6. Schedule Helper now shows in the list:
  7. Pin it so it's easy to find. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then click the pin next to Schedule Helper. Its button (a grey "S") now stays in the toolbar.

Chrome sometimes pops up a message about "developer mode extensions". Just close it, the helper keeps working. If you ever move the folder, do steps 3 to 5 again.

Updating from an older version: extract the new zip over the old folder (say yes to replacing the files), then on chrome://extensions click the round arrow on the Schedule Helper card and refresh Kronos.

B. Every week: load the schedule

  1. Make and Post the schedule in HotSchedules like you always do.
  2. Download the Weekly Roster from HotSchedules. It's the same file you sent Joseph, named something like Weekly_Roster_10052026_10112026. It can be a .csv or an Excel file; both work.
  3. Click the Schedule Helper button in Chrome's toolbar. A page opens.
  4. Click Choose File and pick the Weekly Roster you just downloaded.
  5. Check the list:
    • Week starting should be that week's Monday.
    • Each day lists its shifts. "next day" means the shift ends after midnight. "double" means that person works twice that day.
    • Names are written the Kronos way, "Last, First". If a name is wrong (two last names, a nickname), open Names and jobs as Kronos spells them and fix it there. The helper remembers your fixes for next week.
    • Shifts marked job change are shifts where someone works a different job than usual (for example a server on Bar). See section E.
    • Untick any shift that shouldn't go into Kronos.
  6. Click Use this schedule.
  7. Click Open Kronos, sign in like normal and go to the Schedule Planner. The Schedule Helper box appears in the bottom right corner. To move it, drag its green top bar. To make it smaller, click –.

C. Copy mode (simplest: you do every click)

  1. Open the Copy tab. It shows one shift at a time.
  2. In Kronos, open Add Shift for that person and day (double-click the empty spot on the grid, or right-click it and pick Add Shift). The add-shift panel opens on the side (with a 1-hour shift filled in: replace the times).
  3. Click Copy next field, click the matching box in the panel, and press Ctrl+V to paste. Do the same for each value. You can also click any single value to copy just that one.
  4. Press Apply in the panel and Save at the top, then click ✓ Done, next shift.
  5. Your ticks are saved. If you close Chrome, you'll be back where you stopped next time. The list at the bottom shows every shift and lets you jump to any of them.

If Kronos wants times or dates written differently (for example 17:00 instead of 5:00 PM), change them under Text formats at the bottom of the Copy tab.

D. Auto-fill (the helper types for you, after you OK it)

First, show it once. The helper has to watch you add one shift before it knows how your Kronos works. In the Schedule Planner you add a shift from an empty spot on the grid (in that person's row, under that day): double-click it, or right-click it and pick Add Shift. A panel opens on the side, you fill it in, press Apply, then Save at the top of the planner. That's exactly what to show it.

  1. Open the Show me once tab. Pick a normal shift (one without ⚠) and click Start watching.
  2. On the grid, find that person's row (the names down the left side) and that day (the dates across the top). Click an empty spot where they meet. Don't click on a shift that's already there. If the helper box is in the way, click its – button to shrink it (click it again later to open it) or drag it aside.
  3. The add-shift panel opens on the side. Kronos puts in a 1-hour shift where you clicked; that's fine. Leave Regular as it is. Click into Start Time, select what's there and type the start; do the same in End Time. Press Apply in the panel, then Save in the planner's top bar.
  4. Click I saved it - done in the helper. You'll see a list of what you did. Check that:
    • The first step says Click the empty spot on the grid for this shift's person and day, with a ✓ after the name and date you clicked.
    • Each value you typed has the right label, like Start Time and End Time.
    • The Apply click is marked this is the Save / Apply button (closes the form), and the top-bar Save click is in the list after it.
    Then click Save these steps.

Did you show it before with the old version (the "a spot with no words" warning)? On the load page click Forget "Show me once", then show it again like this.

Then let it fill the week:

  1. Open the Auto-fill tab and click Start.
  2. It shows one day at a time. Shifts that already seem to be in Kronos are marked ⚠ already there? and unticked. Untick anything else you don't want, then click Add the ticked shifts.
  3. For each shift it clicks the empty spot in that shift's own person's row and day, fills in the panel, presses Apply and Save, at about the speed a person types. Leave the mouse and keyboard alone while it works. The red Stop button stops it whenever you want.
  4. If you picked "asks me before every shift" on the load page, it stops before each Save so you can check the shift first.
  5. If it can't find something, it stops and asks you. See section F.
  6. When it's done, look over the week in Kronos before you finish.

E. Job-change shifts (a server working Bar)

When you add a shift, Kronos fills in the person's usual job. So if a server has a bartending shift, the job has to be changed to Bar for that one shift. The helper works out everyone's usual job (the job they work most that week) and marks every shift with a different job.

  • Copy mode: those shifts show a red box, for example ⚠ BARTENDER shift – change the job for this one, and the Job button is outlined in red. Click it to copy the job, then change the job in Kronos for that shift.
  • Auto-fill: show it once how you change the job (it's part 2 of the Show me once tab):
    1. Under Part 2: job-change shifts, pick a shift and click Show me on this shift.
    2. The helper clicks the grid spot, fills in the times and waits before Save. Change the job in the side panel the way you normally do (usually Transfer Employee, then pick the job, then Apply in the transfer box). Don't press Save.
    3. Click I changed it – done. Check that the step where you picked the job says Job, then click Remember these steps. It asks before saving that shift.
    After that, auto-fill changes the job only on the ⚠ shifts. If it can't find the job box or menu, it stops and asks you. If you haven't shown it yet, it stops at the first job-change shift and lets you show it there, or change the job yourself.
  • Wrong usual job? If Kronos has someone under a different main job than the helper guessed, open Names and jobs as Kronos spells them on the load page and pick their Usual job in Kronos. It's remembered for next week.

F. If it can't find the spot or a box

Nothing is lost when this happens. The helper stops, says what it was looking for, and gives you three buttons:

  • I did it: do that one step yourself (for example click the empty spot for that person and day), then press it. The helper carries on from there.
  • Try again: use this after scrolling the grid so that person's row shows.
  • Skip this shift: leaves it for you to add by hand (Copy mode helps).

Common reasons:

  • The person's row isn't on the screen. Scroll the grid until you can see their name, then press Try again. A filter or the "View by" setting can hide people too.
  • The day isn't on the screen. Make sure the planner shows the whole week you loaded (the dates along the top).
  • The name is spelled differently in Kronos. Fix it on the load page under Names and jobs as Kronos spells them, click Use this schedule and refresh Kronos.
  • That day is full (no free space left in the row). Click a free bit of the row yourself and press I did it.

The debug export button. If the list after Show me once shows a warning like "This click has no name I can find again", or the grid step names the wrong person or day, there's a small debug export button next to that step.

  1. Click debug export. Chrome saves a small file called schedule-helper-debug.txt in your Downloads folder.
  2. Send that file to Joseph (Discord is fine), with a screenshot of the helper box if you can.

The file only describes the spot you clicked on the Kronos page: what kind of box it is, its labels, and the row and date it sits in. There's no password in it and no other staff names. You can open it in Notepad to check before you send it.

G. If something else looks wrong

  1. The box doesn't show up on Kronos: refresh the page (F5). Make sure you're on the Schedule Planner, and that Schedule Helper is switched on at chrome://extensions.
  2. A shift is marked "job change" but shouldn't be (or the other way round): set that person's Usual job in Kronos on the load page, click Use this schedule again, and refresh Kronos.
  3. A name or job is wrong: fix it on the load page (step B5), click Use this schedule again, and refresh Kronos.
  4. Shifts are on the wrong days: check that Week starting is the Monday of that week.
  5. Auto-fill typed into the wrong box, or Kronos changed its panel: click Stop and delete the wrong shift in Kronos yourself. Then, on the load page, click Forget "Show me once" and show it again. You can always use Copy mode instead.
  6. Late shifts: a shift ending at 12:00 AM or 1:00 AM ends the next day. Check that the first one looks right in Kronos.
  7. "Already there?" is only a guess from what's on the screen. You decide.
  8. To start the week over: on the load page, click Untick every shift.
  9. Still stuck? Take a screenshot and send it to Joseph.
